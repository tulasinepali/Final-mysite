About Bikram Sambat (B.S.) Calendar

The Bikram Sambat (विक्रम संवत्) is the official civil calendar of Nepal. It is approximately 56 years and 8 months ahead of the standard international Gregorian calendar (A.D.). The Nepali New Year commences on the 1st of Baishakh (normally mid-April).

Unlike the Gregorian calendar, where month lengths are fixed except for leap years, Bikram Sambat months vary each year between 29 and 32 days based on solar astronomical movements through zodiac signs (Sankranti).
