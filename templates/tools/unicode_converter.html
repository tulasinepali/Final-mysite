Understanding Preeti & Nepali Unicode

For decades, Nepali government offices, publishers, and newspapers have used legacy fonts like Preeti, Kantipur, and Fontasy Himali. However, legacy fonts require the recipient to have the specific font installed on their computer, otherwise the text renders as gibberish (e.g., g]kfn instead of नेपाल).

Unicode is the international computing standard that assigns a unique, universal code point to every Nepali character. Text in Unicode displays properly across all smartphones, social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, WhatsApp), web browsers, and search engines without installing any extra software.
